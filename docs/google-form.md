# Google Form specification

## Required questions

1. Full name
2. CBS email address
3. Profile photograph
4. Country or region of origin
5. Previous employment
6. Undergraduate institution
7. Desired industry
8. Hobbies
9. Age (optional)
10. LinkedIn profile URL (optional)
11. Consent to display the submitted information and photograph

## Photograph settings

- File upload question
- One file
- Image files only
- Clear portrait photograph
- Recommended maximum size: 10 MB

Respondents must sign in to a Google account to upload a file. Test the completed form with a classmate account before distribution.

## Consent language

> I consent to having the information and photograph submitted through this form displayed in the access-controlled MBAxMS Class Profile Directory for the class project. I understand that I may request correction or removal of my profile.

Do not request dates of birth, passwords, private telephone numbers, or other information that is unnecessary for the directory.
